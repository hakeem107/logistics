Our services

Multi-field logistics, one accountable partner.

Every service line is run by logistics specialists — so whether your freight needs a load board, a technology stack, or a right-sized small business program, you get depth, not a generalist.

01 — Load board

Load Board & Freight Matching

Capacity on demand. Post your load or your recurring lanes, and our dispatch team works the boards and our own carrier network to cover it — fast, compliant, and priced to the market.

  • Same-day coverage on posted loads
  • FTL, LTL, and partial load matching
  • Rate negotiation with market benchmarks
  • Carrier vetting on every single match

02 — IT logistics

IT Logistics

Your supply chain shouldn't be a spreadsheet. We bring shippers and carriers onto connected systems — tracking, documents, and data that flow from pickup to delivery and into your reporting.

  • Real-time shipment tracking links
  • TMS and platform integrations & EDI
  • Digital documentation — BOL, POD, invoices
  • Lane and spend analytics you can act on

03 — Small business

Small Business Logistics

You don't need a freight department — you need ours. Small businesses get the same carrier network, the same tracking, and the same rates we negotiate at scale, without long-term contracts or minimums.

  • No volume minimums or lock-in contracts
  • Guidance on packaging, mode, and timing
  • LTL consolidation that cuts per-unit cost
  • Scalable as your order volume grows

04 — Carriers

Carrier Sourcing & Compliance

We hire the trucking companies behind your freight — and we're strict about it. Every carrier in our network is checked for operating authority, insurance, and safety performance before they touch a load.

  • FMCSA authority and insurance verification
  • Safety score and performance history review
  • Onboarding paperwork and W-9 management
  • Ongoing scorecards and quick-pay options

05 — Funds

Freight Audit & Cost Control

Freight spend leaks money through duplicate invoices, accessorials, and unbenchmarked rates. We audit every line and report savings in plain English.

  • Line-by-line invoice and accessorial audits
  • Rate benchmarking before every booking
  • Mode and consolidation optimization
  • Quarterly savings reporting
